Checklist
S/No. Checklist Yes No N/A Remarks
1 Is the ground where the scaffold is to be erected firm and not water-logged?
2 Have any open excavation near to the scaffold which may endanger its stability or access to it, been noted and allowed for?
3 Have precautions been taken to allow for any possible future excavations which may endanger the scaffold or access to it?
4 Have any overhead hazards such as power transmission lines, telephone wires, etc., in the vicinity of the scaffold been noted and allowed for?
5 Do you know the purpose for which the scaffold is to be used Is it for: A. Light duty? B. Medium duty? C. Heavy duty?
6 Are the erectors wearing safety helmets that cannot slip whilst being worn?
7 Are the erectors supplied with personal safety protection devices like safety harness, life line, etc., and do they use the devices?
8 Is there a qualified scaffold supervisor around to supervise the erection of the scaffold?
9 Have warning notices stating “CAUTION: DO NOT USE THE SCAFFOLD” been fixed in prominent positions while erection is in progress?
10 Are you familiar with the requirements of this Code of Practice, the WSH Act 2006, WSH (Construction) Regulation 2007, WSH (Scaffold) Regulations 2011?
11 Where the scaffold is not erected onto a solid foundation, have sole-plates been placed and are they of the correct thickness and lengths?
12 Have all standards been fitted with baseplates?
13 Have foot-ties been fitted as near to the ground or basing level as possible?
14 Are the standards placed at their correct distances from each other and from the structure they are servicing?
15 Are standards plumb but a slight lean-in towards the structure they are servicing?
16 Are connections between standards and ledgers made with right-angle couplers?
17 Are transoms at standards connected whether to the standards or to the ledgers with right-angle couplers?
18 Are diagonal bracings connected either to projecting transoms with right-angle couplers or to the standards with swivel couplers? Note: Do not connect diagonal bracing to ledgers.
19 Are ledgers bracings connected to the ledgers with right-angle couplers or to the standards with swivel couplers?
20 Are fittings at standards grouped as closely together as possible?
21 Are tie-ins of correct design being fixed in correct positions as the scaffold rises and not more than two lifts behind?
22 Are ledgers and transoms truly horizontal and correctly spaced, particularly at decking-out level?
23 Are timber boards for decking-out of correct thickness, length and width for the intended purpose of the scaffold?
24 Are the boards laid close together side-by-side and butting close to each other to make a level deck?
25 If over-lapping boards are used, are the over-laps at least 300mm and fillet pieces fitted of adequate cross-section so that the change in level is gradual?
26 Do the boards over-sail their last support by more than four-times their thickness or not less than 50mm?
27 Are precautions being taken to hold down decking against high winds?
28 Are toeboards provided and securely fastened in position?
29 Are guardrails fitted and connected to standards?
30 Are ledgers and diagonal bracings connected end-to-end using either sleeve couplers or joint-pins with butt-straps?
31 Are standards connected end-to-end with either sleeve couplers or joint pins?
32 Are end-to-end connections in members staggered in adjacent bays and adjacent pairs so that joints in similar members do not occur close together?
33 If the scaffold is erected over a public access has a safety net of close-mesh wire or a fan of boards been erected to prevent articles and debris dislodged from the scaffold falling on to the public access?
34 Are ladders provided to give access to every working platform?
35 Are the ladders rising not more than 9m vertically between landings?
36 Are the ladders projecting at least 1m above the level of the landing?
37 Are the ladders securely lashed to the scaffold at top and bottom so that no accidental displacement can take place?
38 Are the ladders firmly supported at their base so that no settlement can take place?
39 Is the angle of the ladder approximately four units vertically to one unit horizontally?
40 Is the scaffold connected to any other temporary structure?
41 Has a Professional Engineer been consulted on whether the removal of the connecting temporary structure will affect the strength or stability of the scaffold?
42 Have any hazards that may affect workers on the scaffold been noted?
